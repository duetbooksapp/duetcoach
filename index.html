Questions

Frequently asked

Is DuetCoach affiliated with AAA?▶
No. DuetCoach is an independent training tool built by Steve Maxim, an AAA Life Specialist. It is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or sponsored by The Auto Club Group or any of its subsidiaries. The scripts are based on common sales frameworks and are designed for personal practice only.
How do API keys work?▶
Pro features (Claude AI mode, Voice mode) use third-party AI services. You'll need free accounts at Anthropic (for Claude) and ElevenLabs (for voice). The app walks you through setup with videos. Your keys stay in your browser only — they're never sent to our servers. Typical usage costs $5-15/month combined for both services.
Can I cancel anytime?▶
Yes. Cancel through the Stripe customer portal anytime to stop future charges. Your trial doesn't bill until day 7. After that, you're billed monthly until you cancel. See our Refund Policy for details.
What about my recordings? Are they private?▶
Recordings are stored locally on your device only. They never get uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone. You can download them at any time. We recommend keeping practice recordings for personal use only — see our Terms of Service.
Will this work on my phone?▶
DuetCoach is currently optimized for desktop Chrome. Voice mode and microphone features have the best support there. The app technically works on mobile, but for full reliability we recommend desktop.
What if I forget my PIN?▶
There's a "Forgot PIN" link on the sign-in screen. It will email Steve, who can manually verify and reset your PIN. We don't store your PIN in plain text — it's hashed for security.
Will DuetCoach guarantee I'll close more deals?▶
No. DuetCoach is a practice tool, not a guarantee. Like any training, the results depend on how consistently and thoughtfully you use it. The agents who practice daily and apply what they learn will see results. There are no guarantees on sales outcomes.
