Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DuetCoach ("we," "us," "the Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application at duetbooksapp.github.io/duetcoach.

Privacy at a glance: We collect only what's needed to make the app work. Your API keys, recordings, and notes stay on your device. We don't sell, share, or monetize your data. Your scoring data syncs to Firebase so it's available across devices.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name — used as your account identifier
  • Your email — used for account recovery and important service communications
  • A PIN — stored only as a SHA-256 cryptographic hash. We cannot recover or read your actual PIN.

Practice Data

To provide the core features of the Service, we store:

  • Your practice session history (date, scenario, scores, transcripts)
  • Your personal session notes
  • Your settings and preferences (default mode, daily goal, etc.)
  • Your streak data and badges earned

Payment Information (Pro Tier Only)

If you subscribe to Pro, payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We do not store, see, or have access to your credit card information. Stripe maintains its own privacy practices, available at stripe.com/privacy.

We receive limited information from Stripe to identify your subscription status (such as your subscription ID and email address).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect or Store on Our Servers

The following data stays only on your device in your browser's local storage and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Your API keys (Anthropic, ElevenLabs) — these are stored in your browser only and used to call those services directly from your browser
  • Session audio recordings — recordings are auto-downloaded to your device and never uploaded anywhere
  • Browser session data — cookies and session tokens stay local

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the Service
  • Authenticate your account when you sign in
  • Sync your practice data across your devices
  • Generate scoring and statistics for your sessions
  • Communicate important updates, billing notices, and support replies
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms

4. How Data Is Stored

Your account and practice data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database, a service provided by Google. Firebase data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Firebase's privacy practices are available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Third-Party Services

Pro tier features connect to third-party AI services using your own API keys. When you use Pro features:

  • Your messages are sent directly from your browser to Anthropic and ElevenLabs
  • Their privacy policies apply — see Anthropic's privacy policy and ElevenLabs' privacy policy
  • We do not see, intercept, or store the content of these requests on our servers

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties except:

  • With service providers we use to operate the Service (Firebase for storage, Stripe for payments) — only as needed to provide their service to you
  • To comply with legal obligations, court orders, or government requests
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users

7. Team Visibility (Leaderboard)

If you participate in the team leaderboard feature, the following information is visible to other DuetCoach users:

  • Your name
  • Your current and longest practice streaks
  • Your total session count and weekly count
  • Whether you practiced today

Your individual scores, transcripts, notes, and personal data are private and never shared with other users.

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your data via the History, Stats, and Settings tabs
  • Export your data via Settings → "Export History"
  • Delete your data via Settings → "Clear All Data"
  • Request account deletion by emailing steve.maxim@gmail.com

If you are a California resident (CCPA) or in the European Economic Area (GDPR), you have additional rights including the right to access, correct, port, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

9. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data from our active databases within 30 days, though backups may retain data for up to 90 days.

10. Children's Privacy

DuetCoach is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can remove it.

11. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data, including:

  • SHA-256 hashing of PINs (we never store plaintext PINs)
  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Firebase's built-in security and encryption
  • API keys stored only in your browser, not on our servers

However, no online service is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated via email.

13. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Contact: steve.maxim@gmail.com
