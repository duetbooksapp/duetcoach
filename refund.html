Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026
Quick summary: 7-day free trial, no charge until day 8. Cancel anytime to stop future billing. Once a billing period is paid, no refunds for unused time.

Free Trial

All new Pro subscriptions include a 7-day free trial. During the trial:

  • You have full access to all Pro features
  • Your card will not be charged until day 8
  • You can cancel anytime during the trial at no cost
  • If you cancel before the trial ends, you will not be billed

Recurring Billing

If you do not cancel before your trial ends:

  • Your card will be charged $19.00 USD on day 8
  • Your subscription will renew monthly thereafter at $19.00 USD
  • Renewal occurs automatically on the same day each month
  • You will receive an email receipt from Stripe for each charge

How to Cancel

You can cancel your subscription at any time:

  1. Sign in to DuetCoach
  2. Go to Settings → Subscription
  3. Click "Manage Subscription" — this opens the Stripe customer portal
  4. Click "Cancel subscription"

Alternatively, you can email steve.maxim@gmail.com with the subject "Cancel Subscription" and include the email address used to subscribe.

What Happens When You Cancel

  • You retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period. If you cancel on the 15th and your billing date is the 30th, you have Pro access until the 30th.
  • After the billing period ends, your account reverts to the Free tier
  • You keep all your practice history, notes, and stats — only Pro features become unavailable
  • You can re-subscribe anytime to regain Pro access

Refund Policy

Once a billing period has been charged, that period is non-refundable. This policy applies because:

  • You have access to the Service for the entire billing period
  • The 7-day free trial gives you full opportunity to evaluate before being charged
  • You can cancel anytime to prevent future charges

Exceptions to the Refund Policy

We may issue refunds in the following exceptional circumstances:

  • Duplicate charges — if you were charged twice for the same billing period due to a system error
  • Service unavailability — if the Service was unavailable for a substantial portion of your billing period due to our error (not third-party API issues)
  • Unauthorized charges — if your account was used without your authorization (please report immediately)
  • Goodwill exceptions — at our sole discretion in rare cases

To request a refund under these circumstances, email steve.maxim@gmail.com within 30 days of the charge with details of your situation.

Third-Party API Costs Are Not Refundable

Pro tier features use your own API keys for Anthropic and ElevenLabs. Charges incurred from those third-party services are billed by them directly to you and are not refundable by DuetCoach. If you have questions about API charges, contact those providers.

Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank or card issuer (chargeback) instead of contacting us, your account may be suspended or terminated. We strongly encourage you to contact us first — most issues can be resolved quickly without involving your bank.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The version that applies to your subscription is the one in effect at the time of your subscription. Material changes will be communicated via email.

Contact

Questions about billing, refunds, or cancellation? Contact: steve.maxim@gmail.com
